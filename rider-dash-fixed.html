How the Scoring Works
Signal logic, risk thresholds, and explainability methodology
Risk classification — normalised thresholds
Risk bands are based on each rider's breach score, normalised 0–100 within the current upload using min-max scaling (highest raw scorer = 100, others scaled proportionally). Note: thresholds are relative to the current dataset — a rider's band may differ across uploads if the peer group changes. This is by design for intra-cohort prioritisation.
Score range Risk band Interpretation
51 – 100 High Risk Two or more signals confirmed. Immediate review required.
20 – 50 Medium Risk At least one significant signal detected. Monitor closely.
0 – 19 Low Risk No signals or weak signals only. Normal behaviour.
Signal weights
Signal Field Weight Trigger condition
Fast NC is_fast_nc 10 pts × count NC within 20 min of OFD
Late-night NC is_late_nc 5 pts × count NC after 9 PM
Burst pattern is_burst 25 pts 3+ NCs within 10 min window
Hub deviation z_score_penalty z × 10 pts Rider NC rate above hub mean
Scoring formula
raw_score = (fast_nc_count × Wfast) + (late_nc_count × Wlate) + (is_burst × Wburst) + max(0, hub_z_score × Whub_dev).
Then min-max normalised: rider_breach_score = round(raw / max_raw × 100).
Explainability tags
Each rider row shows colour-coded tags explaining exactly which signals fired and with what evidence. Generated from rider-level aggregates. Example tags:

3 fast NCs · avg 6 min  2 late-night NCs  burst · 3+ NCs within 10 min  hub outlier · z=2.1
Export
The Export CSV button on the Rider Breach Scores page downloads exactly the riders visible in the current filtered view — same sort order, same filters applied. Columns include Rider ID, Name, Hub, Orders, NC Rate, Breach Score, Risk Band, and full explainability text.
Disclaimer
Probabilistic scoring model for manual review prioritisation only. Scores do not constitute a disciplinary determination. All flagged cases must be independently verified before any action is taken.
